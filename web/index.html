Missing attendance entries by center

Most-affected classes

Missing attendance entries (date · class with no entry)

An entry is flagged when no attendance was entered for that class on a date the center was in session (another class recorded), excluding holidays. Classes with some entries are checked on their weekly cadence within their active window. Registered classes with active students but zero entries are also listed and marked missing for every in-session date at that center. Partial entries where some students were left unmarked are not counted here. For centers where certain grades meet only on specific dates (e.g. STEP 11–12 at Dallas / Lewisville), add a session_dates.csv in the same year folder so other Saturdays are not treated as missing or absent.
