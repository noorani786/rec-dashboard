Most-affected classes

Missing attendance entries (date · class with no entry)

An entry is flagged when no attendance was entered for that class on a date the center was in session (another class recorded), excluding holidays. Classes with some entries are checked on their weekly cadence within their active window. Registered classes with active students but zero entries are also listed and marked missing for every in-session date at that center. Partial entries where some students were left unmarked are not counted here.
